BERLIN / FIELD ATLAS

The city, at its own scale.

Explore 112 architectural stops across 77 km² of central Berlin. Official Geoportal Berlin LoD2 building surfaces preserve their metre-based positions. OpenStreetMap supplies roads, parks and water. Ground elevations are inferred from building bases; road widths are approximate.

Three landmarks have lightweight interpretive details. Other buildings retain generalized LoD2 roof and wall geometry. This is an architecture guide, not a measured survey or BIM model.

Mark a building as visited to reveal photo-informed exterior colours and schematic surface patterns. These are material studies, not photographic textures or surveyed facades. Photos may show earlier conditions. Visiting changes only the display, not geometry or Rhino downloads.

Neighbourhood colours follow mapped OpenStreetMap Ortsteil boundaries. Rail overlays show U-Bahn and S-Bahn track corridors, including tunnels, rather than individual service routes. Check a transport operator for current services. Layer preferences are saved in this browser. Categories are editorial groups; art museums may appear in both Museums and Galleries. Art spaces and Clubs add nine venue locations, with official visitor links. Venue visits save a marker check without recolouring their host building.

Visit records, label preferences and map style are saved in this browser only. Clearing site data removes them. Reduced-motion preferences disable the material-reveal animation. No decorative people or planting are included.

Fixed orthographic isometric view · drag to pan · scroll to zoom. A lightweight overview preserves mapped footprints. Zoom in for official roof geometry; detailed tiles load in pairs within a memory budget, and offscreen tiles are released. Photographs load when a card opens.
